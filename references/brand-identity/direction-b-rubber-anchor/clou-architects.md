# CLOU Architects

## Behavior observed
clouarchitects.com/projects intro: identity statements cycling in a solid black bar at dead center — "We are architects." → "We are interior designers." → "We are CLOU." — type liquid-morphing between statements while the bar NEVER moves. Then dozens of project-card decks fan out and arrange in a ring ORBITING the black bar. Bilingual En/中文 toggle.

## Why it's a Direction reference
Purest execution of the gravity thesis: identities change, work multiplies, the dense center holds absolutely still — "flexible in form, immovable in essence" as choreography. The cycling-identity mechanism maps directly onto PAULUS's multidisciplinary claim ("We are 3D artists / copywriters / world-builders / We are PAULUS"). Bilingual toggle models cross-cultural confidence.

## Likely technique (hypothesis)
Scroll-choreographed sequence (GSAP ScrollTrigger or similar); project decks as 3D-transformed card stacks (CSS 3D or WebGL) positioned on a ring; type morph likely crossfade + width animation rather than true glyph interpolation.

## Teardown TODO (build phase)
Ring layout math and responsive collapse; whether cards are DOM or WebGL; scroll-hijack degree (check against our no-scroll-jacking principle); intro skip behavior on repeat visits.

## Calibration notes
Only the intro sequence is the reference — inner project pages are conventional. Watch total intro length: ours must respect performance-as-design and let returning users through fast. Mechanism idea (identity cycling) flagged for CD/AD; not to be copied one-to-one.
