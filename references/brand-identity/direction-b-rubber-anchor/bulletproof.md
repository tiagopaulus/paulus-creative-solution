# Bulletproof

## Behavior observed
Dark hero with enormous staggered type — CREATE DESIRE THROUGH DISRUPTION — where gray letterforms fill with moving video on scroll (type as window; words with something alive inside). Hard flip to a light section: "UNRIVALLED CREATIVITY. GLOBAL REACH." with a single pink accent word. Global brand agency (also in Theme 02 pool).

## Why it's a Direction reference
Primary reason (Tiago): breaks the expected form of websites themselves — typography escaping its job, becoming the medium's own meta-interpretation. Maps to Rubber Anchor value #1: "breaks through expected forms." Secondary: typographic mass — confidence in plain declarative sentences at architectural scale; calm swagger's typography solved.

## Likely technique (hypothesis)
Video-in-text via CSS background-clip: text or SVG mask over a <video>; scroll-linked reveal; section flip standard.

## Teardown TODO (build phase)
Clip vs mask approach and mobile performance; video weight/streaming; how the accent-word system is structured for reuse.

## Calibration notes
Take the type-mass and form-breaking mechanics; LEAVE the vocabulary — "disruption/unrivalled" is louder and more agency-cliché than PAULUS's quiet-until-funny voice. Video-fill must obey our no-simultaneous-triggers performance rule.
